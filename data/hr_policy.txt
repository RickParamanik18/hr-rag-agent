HR POLICY DOCUMENT
=================

Company: Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
Effective Date: 08/10/2026
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE
-----------
This HR policy defines the workplace rules, employee responsibilities,
benefits, and procedures of Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. The purpose is to
maintain a professional, respectful, inclusive, safe, and productive
workplace.

2. WORKING HOURS
----------------
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Employees are expected to be punctual and available during working hours.
- Employees are entitled to a one-hour lunch break.
- Remote or flexible work arrangements require manager approval.

3. ATTENDANCE AND LEAVE
-----------------------
- Employees must inform their manager as early as possible if they cannot
  attend work.
- Planned leave should be requested and approved in advance.
- Emergency leave should be reported as soon as reasonably possible.
- Employees receive 18 paid leaves per calendar year, subject to company
  rules and applicable law.
- Unauthorized absence may result in disciplinary action.

4. CODE OF CONDUCT
------------------
Employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues, customers, and business partners respectfully.
- Communicate professionally.
- Follow company policies and lawful instructions.
- Protect company property and confidential information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest and report potential conflicts.
- Maintain professional behavior both in-person and during remote work.

5. EQUAL OPPORTUNITY
--------------------
Nexora Technologies is committed to equal employment opportunity. Employment
decisions are based on qualifications, skills, performance, experience, and
legitimate business requirements.

Discrimination based on legally protected characteristics is not tolerated.

6. ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY
-------------------------
Harassment, sexual harassment, bullying, intimidation, threats, and other
inappropriate workplace behavior are prohibited.

Employees may report concerns to HR, their manager, or the designated
complaints channel. Complaints will be handled fairly and confidentially to
the extent reasonably possible, subject to applicable law and investigation
requirements.

7. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
----------------------------
- Salary is paid monthly according to the employee's employment agreement.
- Statutory deductions and benefits will be handled according to applicable
  Indian laws.
- Eligible employees may receive benefits such as health insurance, paid
  leave, and performance-based incentives according to company policy.
- Benefits may be modified based on business and legal requirements.

8. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
-------------------------
- Employees receive periodic performance feedback.
- Performance goals are established by employees and their managers.
- Employees who are not meeting expectations may receive coaching, training,
  additional support, or a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
- Continued or serious performance issues may result in disciplinary action.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION
--------------------------------------
Employees must protect confidential company, employee, customer, and business
information.

Confidential information must not be disclosed, copied, transferred, or used
without authorization. Employees must follow Nexora's information-security
and data-protection requirements.

10. COMPANY PROPERTY AND IT USAGE
---------------------------------
- Company laptops, accounts, software, networks, and other resources must be
  used responsibly.
- Employees must keep passwords and authentication credentials secure.
- Unauthorized access, data theft, malware installation, or misuse of company
  systems is prohibited.
- Company equipment must be returned when employment ends.

11. REMOTE WORK POLICY
----------------------
Employees working remotely must:
- Maintain agreed working hours and availability.
- Protect company data and equipment.
- Use secure networks and approved applications.
- Attend required meetings.
- Maintain a professional working environment.
- Follow the same performance and conduct standards as office-based employees.

12. HEALTH AND SAFETY
---------------------
Nexora Technologies aims to provide a safe and healthy workplace.

Employees must:
- Follow workplace safety instructions.
- Report accidents, hazards, or unsafe conditions.
- Cooperate with reasonable health and safety procedures.
- Follow emergency and evacuation procedures.

13. GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
-----------------------
Employees may raise workplace concerns with their manager or HR.

A grievance should include relevant facts, dates, people involved, and
supporting information where available. HR will review the matter and take
appropriate action based on the circumstances and applicable law.

14. DISCIPLINARY ACTION
-----------------------
Violation of company policies may result in corrective or disciplinary
action. Depending on the seriousness of the matter, action may include:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Suspension
- Performance improvement measures
- Termination of employment

All disciplinary action will be subject to applicable law and company
procedures.

15. RESIGNATION AND TERMINATION
-------------------------------
- Employees must follow the notice period stated in their employment agreement.
- Employees are expected to complete assigned work during the notice period.
- Employees must provide a proper handover of responsibilities.
- Company property, documents, credentials, and confidential information must
  be returned before departure.
- Final settlement will be processed according to company policy and
  applicable law.

16. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
---------------------
Eligible full-time employees may receive:
- Medical insurance coverage
- Paid annual leave
- Casual/sick leave according to company policy
- Performance reviews
- Learning and development opportunities
- Employee wellness initiatives

Specific benefits depend on the employee's employment agreement and applicable
company policies.

17. POLICY CHANGES
------------------
Nexora Technologies may modify this policy when necessary due to business,
operational, or legal requirements. Employees will be informed of material
changes through official company communication channels.
